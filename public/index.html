Connect another phone

  1. Open this same website on the other phone.
  2. Choose GPS / Device as the tracking method.
  3. Turn on Share location on network map.
  4. Press Start and allow location access.

Each device gets its own colour and marker. Your own device is never drawn twice. Devices that stop sharing disappear automatically after 2 minutes.
